My Whereabouts

Country: Indonesia | City: Depok

Stretching across more than 17,000 emerald islands, Indonesia is a breathtaking tapestry of towering volcanoes, mist-shrouded rainforests, and vibrant cultural heritage. Nestled on the lush southern edge of Greater Jakarta in West Java, Depok offers a distinctive blend of modern urban energy and preserved history. From the tranquil waters of its natural lakes and the ancient canopy of the Pancoran Mas nature reserve—one of Java’s oldest protected forests—to the colonial-era landmarks of Depok Lama, the city reflects the rich heritage and scenic greenery that define the archipelago.
